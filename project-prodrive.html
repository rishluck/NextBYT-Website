About

A concept website design for ProDrive, a premium automotive brand that blends performance engineering with an elevated brand identity for the modern driver.

The goal was to create a website with the same energy and precision as a high-performance vehicle: fast, sharp, and impossible to ignore.

Skills used:

UI Design Web Design Automotive Figma E-Commerce

Engineered for
Speed & Impact

The automotive sector is one of the most visually demanding industries on the web. ProDrive required a design that matched the drama and precision of the vehicles themselves: bold typography, cinematic full-bleed imagery, and a layout that feels as fast as it looks.

The hero section uses an immersive, full-screen approach with a dramatic vehicle shot and minimal copy, letting the product do the talking. As the user scrolls, the page reveals performance specs, a model selector, and a booking/enquiry flow engineered for conversions.

Every hover state, transition, and animation is designed to reinforce the feeling of precision and power. The dark colour scheme, sharp edges, and selective use of accent colour create a premium brand experience that stands out in a competitive market.

🏎️
Cinematic Hero
A full-bleed vehicle hero with layered depth effects pulls visitors in immediately and sets the premium tone before a single word is read.
⚙️
Performance Specs UI
Animated stat counters and clean comparison tables let buyers evaluate performance figures at a glance, reducing research time and increasing confidence.
📍
Test Drive Booking
An integrated booking widget allows potential buyers to schedule a test drive directly, closing the gap between browsing and buying.
