About

A concept website design for Horen Foods, a modern food and beverage brand focused on quality ingredients, bold flavours, and a clean, contemporary identity.

The brief was to create an appetising, conversion-focused online presence that drives product discovery and builds trust with health-conscious consumers.

Skills used:

UI Design Web Design Brand Figma FMCG

Designing for
Appetite & Trust

Horen Foods needed a digital home that did justice to the quality of its products. The design centres on large, clean product photography backed by warm, natural tones that communicate authenticity and freshness, exactly what modern food consumers respond to.

The layout prioritises discovery: a bold hero section that leads with the hero product, followed by a clear brand story, a product grid with quick-add functionality, and social proof woven throughout to drive confidence at every step.

Typography is kept clean and legible with generous white space, letting the food imagery do the heavy lifting, while the structure guides visitors from discovery to purchase in as few clicks as possible.

🍃
Product-First Design
The layout is engineered around the product: large hero imagery, clean product cards, and a minimal palette that puts the food front and centre.
🛒
Conversion Focused
Clear CTAs, trust signals, and a frictionless product discovery flow guide visitors from first impression to purchase with minimal drop-off.
📱
Mobile-First
Designed for how modern food shoppers browse: on their phones. Every section is optimised for thumb-friendly navigation and fast loading.
