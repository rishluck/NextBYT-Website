About

A brand identity and web design concept for Pillar Box — an independent London restaurant taking inspiration from the iconic British pillar box, reimagining classic dining for a modern audience.

The challenge was to build a brand identity that feels instantly recognisable and deeply British, while attracting a contemporary, food-forward crowd.

Skills used:

Brand Identity Logo Design Web Design Figma Hospitality

An Iconic Brand for
an Iconic Dining Experience

The pillar box — a symbol as British as afternoon tea — became the heart of this brand. Its bold signal red, circular form, and heritage feel were translated into a modern restaurant identity that stands out in London's competitive dining landscape without feeling pastiche.

The website centres on food photography and an immersive menu experience. A warm red and cream palette, paired with refined serif typography, creates an atmosphere of relaxed sophistication — the kind of place you want to linger long after the meal is finished.

Online reservations, a curated menu, and a story-led about section work together to make the website feel like a natural extension of walking through the restaurant door for the first time.

🍽️
Menu Experience
A beautifully designed digital menu that reads like a printed one — grouped by course, with seasonal specials highlighted and dietary icons clearly marked.
📅
Reservation Integration
Embedded table booking powered by a third-party reservation system — no redirects, no friction. Guests book without ever leaving the site.
🇬🇧
Heritage Identity
A brand system drawn from British iconography — the pillar box red, traditional typography, and a logo mark that works across menus, signage, and social media.
