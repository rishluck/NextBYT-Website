About

SK Chem Solutions imports and distributes industrial raw materials across Sri Lanka, supplying manufacturers since 2017.

They needed a website that looks as reliable as their supply chain and makes it easy for buyers to get in touch.

Skills used:

Web Design Development B2B SEO Mobile

Connecting industries
with quality

The site leads with a full-screen hero and a clear message: trusted raw materials, reliable supply, doorstep delivery. Two buttons take buyers straight to an enquiry or to the industries served.

Each industry, from rubber and latex to textiles, food, pharma and personal care, gets its own page with the products used and why they matter. Partners, a photo gallery and a quick WhatsApp button round out the experience.

The client's fresh lime-green palette sits against deep charcoal, so calls to action shine and the whole site feels clean and modern.
